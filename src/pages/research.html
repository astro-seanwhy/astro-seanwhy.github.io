Research

My research lies at the intersection of planetary and exoplanetary science, drawing on my dual training in astrophysics and geoscience.

  • Volatile Depletion and the Star–Planet Chemical Connection

    Earth and the Sun share nearly identical refractory compositions but diverge in volatiles. This work measures that difference, extends it to exoplanetary systems, and explains it through planet-formation physics.

  • Interior and Atmospheric Modeling of Rocky Exoplanets

    Planetary bulk composition plays a key role in determining planetary interiors, which in turn exert first-order control on surface and atmospheric evolution.

  • Compositional Exoplanetology of Rocky Worlds

    My next major program will integrate condensation chemistry, planetary accretion, and interior–atmosphere coupling into one self-consistent framework.
