Compositional Exoplanetology of Rocky Worlds

Building on my earlier empirical and individual modeling work, my next major program will integrate condensation chemistry, planetary accretion, and interior–atmosphere coupling into a self-consistent formation and evolution framework of compositional exoplanetology for rocky worlds. It marks a major milestone in my pursuit to understand how chemical origins shape observable planetary diversity and habitability across stellar environments.

Looking further ahead, I would be excited to use the anticipated deeper understanding of the chemical diversity of rocky worlds to probe the origin(s) and prevalence of life in the cosmos.
